Mission Statement

Healthy Food, More Healthcare Options, Federal Grants, and a Stronger Workforce — Building a Booming Economy

Access to healthy food directly impacts healthcare. When people eat better, they rely less on emergency rooms for preventable conditions and more on primary care. This reduces overall healthcare costs while giving Kentuckians more healthcare options, including the choice of an affordable, state-funded plan — without forcing anyone to give up their current insurance.

A healthier population creates a stronger workforce. Fewer sick days, lower medical costs, and better long-term health mean higher productivity across every industry. Businesses save money, workers stay employed, and the state grows stronger without expanding bureaucracy.

I’m a working-class Kentucky veteran, not a career politician. I’m running to bring practical solutions like affordable healthcare, healthy food and water, and honest government back to the people of kentucky

Note: I am a convicted felon who has rebuilt his life and is running openly and honestly for Governor of Kentucky.
